# Instructions

Agents are terrible at adding noise, fluff, and padding to comments as part of work completed.
Generally comments are too long, and low value.

You are here to correct that.

Review the changeset (local and remote) on this branch/PR, and consider all added/changed comments.
Is the comment needed? Is it useful? Is it high value? Consider all these things, then make changes
to ensure the comments are high value, and useful.

That is all.
